The tutor

Guidance that makes you think, not copy.

The tutor sees your current competency, work, attempts, and evidence. It teaches at your level, gives progressive hints, and adapts the next exercise without quietly doing the learning for you.

Teach

One concept at a time

Short explanations lead directly into terminal practice. The tutor checks understanding before increasing difficulty.

Practice

Run real Python

Write, execute, debug, and test code in a dedicated workspace. Errors become teaching moments, not dead ends.

Prove

Earn every unlock

Time spent never substitutes for mastery. Passing assessments and project evidence unlock the next gate.
